### Actual Lesson

- Introduction
    - Today going to talk about ethics
        - Define ethics
    - Almost always in life, never easy answers
    - Different value systems drive different behaviors
        - ex: Your best friend cheated on an exam, but asked you not to tell
            - Value that implies you should tell?
            - Value that implies you should **not** tell?
- Trolley problem
    - Trolley problem and ethics
    - Who's heard of it?
    - Explain basic formulation
- What would you do in this situation?
    - Five people but if throw switch one person
- Important problem in ethics
    - Constantly debated
    - Show Good Place video clip
        - https://youtu.be/DtRhrfhP5b4?si=fzlBgMH6xF7whm_g
- Lots of variation
    - There are 1000 people instead of five
    - The five people are convicted murderers
    - The one person is 98 years old
    - The five people are members of your family
    - The one person will someday cure cancer
    - What is point of variation?
        - Way of making value systems clear
- Introduce `Trial by Trolley`
    - Going to play game
    - Show video
        - https://youtu.be/6oeHHfSoqt8?si=ZOKMPBVZ4i5dnp3x&t=34
        - Quit before her sign of ("I'm Becca Scott") ... there's a bleep swear
    - Show rules
- Play
- Switch to AI
    - More an more AI is being involved in decision making
        - Is that a good or bad thing?
    - Feed answers into ChatBot to see what it would do
    - Drop point at the end:
        - Seems like a game to us, but AI will answer it
