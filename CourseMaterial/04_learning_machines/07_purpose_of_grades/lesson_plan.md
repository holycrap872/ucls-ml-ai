## Essential Questions

- What grading systems best promote individual learning?
- What grading systems best promote societal improvement?

## Lesson Plan

### Setup

- `Discussion Rubric` printed out
    - https://docs.google.com/document/d/1hKjFvPVQ6SnSp94-YfhgT5zULjyhhOHjI3ZZyKh3cKw

### Actual Lesson

- Review
    - Types of learning (supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement)
    - Types of classifiers (bayesian, decision trees)
    - Properties of trees
    - Training set
    - Test set
    - Over-fitting
- Today going to have a discussion
    - Hand out discussion rubric
    - Have read through it and ask any questions
- Write for 5m answering one of the essential questions:
    - What grading practices best promote learning, accomplishment, or societal benefit?
    - How might our understanding of learning change if students designed their own assessment systems?
    - Does understanding the difficulties in assigning grades lead to cynicism?
    - How do the ways we measure learning shape the way we learn, both for humans and machines?
- Ground rules
    - Think of positive examples
    - Listen
- Begin discussion
- Wrap up
    - Ask someone to summarize
    - Ask if there's anything that wasn't said
- Debrief
    - Self-assess on the rubric
    - Answer questions on the back
- Introduce "Teaching Unit"
    - Pair project
    - Must be about something wished we had covered better in Intro to CS
