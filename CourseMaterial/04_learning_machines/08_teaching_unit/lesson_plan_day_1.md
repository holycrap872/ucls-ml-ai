- What is essential information for CS students to know?
- What techniques will help students learn that information best?
- How does human learning compare to machine learning?

### Actual Lesson

- Review
    - Grading discussion
    - ML vocab
        - Supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement
    - Human learning vocab
        - Depends on stage of expertise/age
- Project setup:
    - Come up with a two day unit on topic of your choice for 9th graders
        - One 50m class, one 75m class (in any order)
        - Should be something you wished you had learned/spent more time on
            - e.g., functions, accumulator pattern, lists
    - Create ~20m intro slides/activity and 1.5 day worksheet
        - Short intro time because 9th graders lose interest quickly
            - Give them the information they need and get out
        - PRIMM worksheet
            - Trying to keep students interested/engaged throughout
                - Identify goal: memorization, excitement, appreciation
            - Scaffolding
            - Reflection
            - ... all the other stuff that I think is good
        - Assessment for homework on second day
            - Captures knowledge
            - Rubric to grade student's understanding
    - Teaching progression and explain purpose of each element
    - Work in pairs
- Go over `Teaching Unit Worksheet`:
    -  https://docs.google.com/document/d/19wi7dZ0e7rToo3LFZgKIK9hPGyWPnhlkPbCnl5Trqak
- Go over rubric
    - See `teaching_unit_rubic.docx`
- Have four days plus rest of this class
    - Give basic checkpoints based on day
        - Day 1 Homework: Agree on unit and complete "Unit Lesson Plan"
        - Day 2 Homework: Create assessment and slides
        - Day 3 Homework: 1/2 way through worksheet
        - Day 4 Homework: Complete worksheet (peer review next day)
        - Day 5 Homework: Integrate comments, annotations... ready to present
    - Lots to do, so use ChatBot as aid
        - **Must** document ChatBot usage
    - Five minute check-in with every group at end of each day for feedback from me
- Go!
