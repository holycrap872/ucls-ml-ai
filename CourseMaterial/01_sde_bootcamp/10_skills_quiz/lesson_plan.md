## Essential Questions

- What can I do without AI help?
- How well can I predict my own performance?

## Lesson Plan

The first quiz of the year. Roughly 90% Python programming (drawn from wheaties
content students have been practicing) including writing tests for each
function, plus one multiple-choice shell question and one multiple-choice git
question. Closed-book and no AI assistance, which forces students to confront
how much of the unit's work was theirs versus how much they've been leaning on
AI. Sets up the predict-versus-actual pattern that recurs throughout the year
and seeds the cognitive patterns thread that culminates in Unit 09.

### Setup

- `10_skills_quiz.pdf` printed out
    - **DO NOT POST TO SCHOOLOGY** so harder to use AI
    - See `assessment.md` for format
    - Note: **DO NOT** use the same questions from last year
        - Once done quiz, put questions into proper wheaties worksheet
- Index card / half-sheet per student for predictions

### Actual Lesson

- Frame the quiz (~5m)
    - Tell students this is a medium-stakes quiz
        - Ask: what does "medium-stakes" mean?
        - Discuss: it counts, but it's not designed to break anyone. It's designed to give you (and me) a real signal about where you are.
    - Why a quiz now?
        - You've been working with AI for six weeks
        - The question is: what's yours versus what AI has been doing for you
        - The quiz is closed-book on purpose. That's the most honest way to find out.
- Predict your score (~2m)
    - On the index card: write your name and your predicted score (0-100)
    - This is part of the assessment. You get credit for making a prediction; there's no penalty for the number itself.
    - Predictions come back all year
- Quiz (~35m normal, ~52m extended)
    - ONLY have VSCode open (no browser, no outside help)
    - NO Copilot, no AI, no outside resources
    - Roughly 90% Python (problems should feel familiar from wheaties), with at least one test per function
    - One multiple-choice shell question
    - One multiple-choice git question
    - Reflection on the back due by end of day
- Once done, you may leave
    - Make sure to `git push` solutions
    - Hand in your prediction card

### Homework

- None. Reflection on the back of the quiz is your work for tonight.
