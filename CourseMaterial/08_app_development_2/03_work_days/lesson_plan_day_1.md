## Essential Questions

- How does your project's reality compare with your initial expectations?

## Lesson Plan

### Setup

- Students' pre-mortems from kickoff day returned/accessible

### Actual Lesson

- Pre-mortem revisit (~10m)
    - Pull out your pre-mortem from kickoff day
    - Compare to where you are now
    - What did you predict correctly? What surprised you?
    - One sentence: what would you add to your pre-mortem knowing what you know now?
- Work time
    - Continue building
    - Five minute check-in with every group at end of class

### Homework

- Per group checkpoints
