        - Height of rationalism movement
        - Start from strong foundation and build up
    - Contradictions existed
        - The barber who only shaves people who don't shave themselves
        - The set of all things that contain themselves twice
    - Ultimately failed
        - Self reference leads to contradictions
        - `s = set(); s.add(s)`
    - Pretty much everyone who wrestles with sets seems to go at least slightly insane
        - Dangerous Knowledge documentary
    - Logicomix is a _great_ book about this
        - Graphic novel about Russell's quest for rationality
        - Shows how math connects to philosophy and human experience
        - One of coolest marriages between philosophy and math I've ever encountered
